About

I'm a third-year Computer Science and Engineering student at Daffodil International University, Bangladesh. I'm passionate about Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning, and I'm currently building my foundation in Python and problem solving.

Alongside my studies, I run EDU-LUMINOUS, a Bengali-language YouTube channel where I teach math and physics to SSC and HSC students. Teaching has taught me to break down complex ideas into simple steps, a skill I want to carry into research.

My long-term goals are to publish research papers, pursue higher studies abroad, and become a university lecturer, while also working in AI/ML and eventually starting my own company.
